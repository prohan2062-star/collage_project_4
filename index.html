About the Department

The department has grown from a single BCS batch into a full range of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes, with a strong focus on programming, projects and placements.

Vision

To be a trusted centre of computing education that produces skilled, ethical and employable graduates.

Mission

Deliver practical, up-to-date teaching, support research and innovation, and prepare students for industry and higher studies.

Objectives

Build strong programming fundamentals. Encourage project work. Promote soft skills and teamwork. Connect students with industry.
